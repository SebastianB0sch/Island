> **Owner:** A08 - Architect. **Status:** Gate 3: Gate 2 still passes (`-DAPortalTest`, 188/188), the 28 spawn points now have a schedule/event runtime test (`-DANpcScheduleTest`), and the editor build places one nav bounds volume over each of the ten cells. See `Docs/World/NPC_SCHEDULES.md`.

## 0. Gate 2 implementation

| Piece | File | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Cell geometry | `Tools/dai/assets/interiors.py` | Builds 10 `AssetSet`s (3 meshes each: `Shell`, `Props`, `NoCol`) from `interiors.json` dimensions and room kind. Doors, windows, trim, beds, desks, chairs, bunks, bookshelves, dining table, chandelier, Pharaoh tank and room lights are procedural. |
| Layout stage | `Tools/dai/layout.py::Layout.place_interiors()` | Reads `interiors.json`, exports meshes under `Meshes/Locations/interiors`, appends static actors at `origin_ue`, and converts local light/text/interact sockets to UE world data. Existing island placements are untouched. |
| Pipeline hook | `Tools/build_island.py` | Calls `lay.place_interiors()` after the existing locations. |
| Actor spawning | `DuelAcademyIsland/Content/Python/duel_academy/level.py` | Spawns `ADAInteriorPortal` ×20, `ADASceneAnchor` ×89, `ADANpcSpawnPoint` ×28 and one `ADANpcDirector`, setting properties from the World JSON. `build.py` calls `placer.world_actors()`. |
| Validation | `Tools/world/validate_layout.py` | Checks zone/id references, portal symmetry, interior bounds/size consistency, floors, arrivals, anchors, spawns and opening requirements. |

**Generated cell inventory:** 10 cells / 30 meshes / ~21.9k triangles (shell + props + no-collision). Each cell is 3.0–14.0 m wide, 5.0–10.0 m deep and 3.2–4.2 m high; all are below the island at Z = -30,000 cm.

**Exact generation commands (run outside this environment):**

```powershell
# 1. Rebuild the layout data and export the new interior FBXs.
python Tools/build_island.py layout

# 2. Import everything and rebuild the level (headless editor).
UnrealEditor-Cmd DuelAcademyIsland.uproject -ExecutePythonScript="C:/dev/GX/DuelAcademyIsland/Content/Python/build_island_cli.py"

# Or in the editor: Duel Academy > Build Island.

# 3. Validate the data and simulate every portal route.
python Tools/world/validate_layout.py --strict
python Tools/world/test_interiors.py
```

**Actor spawning details:** the level script reads `Content/Data/World/*.json` directly with `Placer.world_actors()`; no manual actor placement and no `.umap` edits. Every spawned actor receives the `DA_Generated` tag so `clear_generated()` can rebuild it safely. `ADANpcDirector` auto-registers the 28 spawn points on BeginPlay; individual points with `spawn_on_begin_play` spawn immediately, and event-only points wait for `SetActiveEvent`.

## 1. Decision: portal cells vs in-place

| Approach | Used for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **In-place** | Academy lobby, Grand Duel Arena, Card Shop, Slifer cafeteria/dining annex, Ra cafeteria, Duel Gym hall, Abandoned Dorm entrance hall | These interiors already exist in the procedural meshes and are walk-in. Adding geometry would mean rebuilding large exterior shells for no visual gain. |
| **Portal cell** | Academy classroom, exam room, Crowler office, Chancellor office; Slifer player/Banner/Jaden rooms; Ra player room; Obelisk dining/player room | The exterior blocks are solid shells. A portal cell gives a controlled, story-sized room without touching the exterior kit, and keeps the level regeneration deterministic. |
| **Merged** | Slifer/Ra/Obelisk common rooms, arena backstage, harbor office, ocean shoreline | The existing room or zone already serves the beat; a second room would be empty most of the year. |

**Cell placement:** all cells are in a dedicated block centred at UE `(300000, 300000, -30000)` cm, 2,000–2,500 cm apart on a grid. That is below the sea floor and above the level kill plane (`-50000`), so no terrain, ocean or sky light leaks in. Cells are ordinary level geometry: no streaming, no loading screen, no persistent tick. The camera fades for 0.35 s and the portal teleports when the screen reaches black; the return portal is suppressed until the player leaves its trigger.

## 4. Lighting / collision / nav requirements

### Lighting
- Each cell gets **2–4 static/movable point lights** (warm ceiling lamps, one cool window fill). No directional sun inside the cell block.
- Emissive materials only for signs, tank glow, stage edge and card-shop displays; do not rely on emissive for room illumination.
- Max 1 shadow-casting light per cell; the rest are unshadowed fill. This keeps the interior block cheap while the exterior sun is still active.
- The block is below the landscape; verify no sky-light bleed at the cell seams. If a seam leaks, add a ceiling/roof slab (the room shell already closes it).

### Collision
- Floor and walls: complex collision from the generated mesh (`SM_Interior_*`), never `none`.
- Props: simple/complex collision; desks, beds and counters must block the player but not create nav islands in the door path.
- Portal triggers: `UBoxComponent`, `QueryOnly`, pawn overlap only, no nav affect. Trigger depth 60–120 cm; do not overlap the arrival point.
- Door leaves are decorative; the portal trigger is authoritative. No physics doors in this pass.

### Nav
- **Implemented in the build:** `level.py::Placer.interior_nav()` spawns one `NavMeshBoundsVolume` per cell from `interiors.json`, labelled `Nav_<cell_id>` in `Gameplay/nav` and tagged `DA_Generated`. Each volume covers `bounds_ue` with a 150 cm XY margin and 250 cm Z headroom (so the whole floor-to-ceiling shell is included). `build.py` saves the level after `world_actors()`, and `clear_generated()` removes the old volumes on rebuild.
- Rebuild navigation once after the editor build (`Build > Build Paths` or `-nav`); the data/volume placement is deterministic.
- Keep a 1.2 m clear path from the exit trigger to the room centre; no desk/bed within 80 cm of the door.
- NPC spawn points use `SpawnZOffset = 90 cm` (capsule half-height) so `ADANpcSpawnPoint` actors sit on the floor.
- Run a nav reachability check per cell: exit trigger → every spawn anchor → exit trigger. A cell with no reachable path fails the interior test.

### Performance budget

| Budget | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Interior transition | < 2 s | Target 0.7 s: 0.35 s fade out + teleport + 0.35 s fade in. No level load. |
| Per-cell draw calls | ≤ 12 | Merge trim/props by material; reuse the kit. |
| Per-cell lights | ≤ 6 | 1 shadow caster max. |
| Per-cell triangles | ≤ 15k | Kit rooms, not hero archviz. |
| Permanent tick cost | 0 | Portals/spawn points/anchors are event-driven; director applies schedules only. |
| Memory | < 50 MB for 10 cells | Shared materials/meshes; no unique textures per room. |

## 6. Generation steps (Gate 2 implementation)

1. **Cell geometry** — `Tools/dai/assets/interiors.py` builds each cell from its `size_m`, `kind` and theme. It reuses `MeshBuilder.room`, `box`, `cylinder`, `beam`, `torus`, `at` and the existing palette. Three meshes per cell: `Interior_<id>_Shell`, `_Props`, `_NoCol`.
2. **Export and place** — `Layout.place_interiors()` reads `Content/Data/World/interiors.json`, exports the FBXs to `SourceAssets/Meshes/Locations/interiors`, appends `static` entries at `origin_ue`, and converts local `light`, `text`, `interact`, `audio` and `emitter` sockets into the layout arrays. Existing island placements are not touched.
3. **Pipeline hook** — `Tools/build_island.py` calls `lay.place_interiors()` after `lay.place_locations()`.
4. **Actor spawning** — `level.py::Placer.world_actors()` reads `portals.json`, `anchors.json` and `spawns.json`, spawns `ADAInteriorPortal`, `ADASceneAnchor`, `ADANpcSpawnPoint` and one `ADANpcDirector`, and sets their properties. `build.py` calls it after the existing NPC placement.
5. **Door leaves/steps** — every cell shell has a door leaf, frame, handle and exterior step on its +Y wall. The paired portal is at the interior side of that door; return portals are always unlocked.
6. **Lock behavior** — entrance portals carry `required_flag`; `bFailClosedWhenFlagQueryMissing` stays false until A02 binds `ADAInteriorPortal::FlagQuery`. Story can also call `SetLocked()` at runtime.
7. **Regenerate** — run the two commands in §0. The island exterior must be unchanged apart from the new `static`, `lights`, `texts`, `interacts` and world actors.
8. **Validate** — `python Tools/world/validate_layout.py --strict`; run `-DAPortalTest` in PIE (all 20 portals, ten entrances, classroom return, screenshot).
9. **Nav bounds** — `level.py::Placer.interior_nav()` places one `NavMeshBoundsVolume` over every cell during the same editor build; no hand-placed volume and no `.umap` edit. Rebuild nav after the build and measure performance. The current cell budget is ~21.9k triangles for all 10 rooms and 2–4 point lights per room.
